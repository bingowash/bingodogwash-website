Delivery Policy

How fulfilment works for products shown in the Bingo Dog Wash online shop.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Bingo direct checkout orders

Products labelled “Bingo direct checkout” are ordered and paid for through Bingo Dog Wash. Some are fulfilled by a supplier on our behalf. The order review and secure checkout show the items and total you will be charged before you pay.

We do not publish a single delivery charge or delivery time that applies to every direct-checkout product. If a delivery cost or estimate is not shown for your order, please contact us before paying so we can confirm the available fulfilment information. We will not add an undisclosed charge after payment.

Partner and affiliate products

Products labelled as Amazon, Etsy, eBay, affiliate, marketplace or external supplier products are sold and fulfilled by the named third-party seller, not from Bingo Dog Wash inventory. Their product page or checkout provides the applicable delivery destinations, charges and estimates. Their delivery policy applies.

Delivery problems

For a Bingo direct order, email info@bingodogwash.com, call 07743 316855, or use our Contact page. For a partner order, contact the seller shown on the order confirmation.

Physical dog wash

The first physical Bingo Dog Wash location is still in development and is not currently open. Wash credits are not delivered products and can be used when the first location opens.
