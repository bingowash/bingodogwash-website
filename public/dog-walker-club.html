Is membership free?

Yes. Applying to the Bingo Dog Walker Directory is free.

Who can apply?

Professional dog walkers and local pet professionals can apply. The initial Founding 100 campaign is for eligible dog walkers.

Do I need a registered business?

No, but you should provide professional details so Bingo Dog Wash can review your application fairly.

How are Founding Members selected?

Founding Member status is only granted after approval and is limited to the first 100 eligible approved dog walkers.

How does the lifetime discount work?

Eligible Founding Members may receive a member discount configured by Bingo Dog Wash and subject to programme terms.

When will my profile appear publicly?

Your profile appears only after application approval and admin publication review.

Will my email address or phone number be displayed?

No. Private email addresses and phone numbers are not shown publicly.

How will Bingo Dog Wash use my information?

Your information is used to review your application, manage membership, administer benefits and handle professional enquiries securely.
