Our Journey

Better bath days for dogs—and their humans.

Bingo Dog Wash was created after seeing how difficult, messy and time-consuming washing a dog at home can be.

Our mission is simple: make quality dog care more accessible through clean, easy-to-use self-service wash stations and a trusted online shop for everyday essentials.

We’re now working towards launching our first Bingo Dog Wash location in the UK. Every supplier conversation, product test and community message takes us one step closer.
